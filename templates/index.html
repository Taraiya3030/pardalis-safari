Our Safari Destinations

Serengeti National Park

The Serengeti is renowned for its endless plains, extraordinary wildlife, and the spectacular Great Wildebeest Migration. Guests have the opportunity to witness lions, elephants, cheetahs, leopards, buffaloes, giraffes, zebras, and countless other species in their natural habitat.

Ngorongoro Conservation Area

Known as Africa's Garden of Eden, the Ngorongoro Crater offers one of the highest concentrations of wildlife on Earth. Visitors may encounter the endangered black rhinoceros alongside elephants, lions, hippos, hyenas, and thousands of grazing animals.

Tarangire National Park

Famous for its giant baobab trees and large elephant herds, Tarangire provides spectacular game viewing and incredible birdlife throughout the year.

Lake Manyara National Park

This beautiful park is known for its tree-climbing lions, flamingos, lush groundwater forests, and breathtaking scenery that stretches from the Great Rift Valley escarpment to the lake itself.

Arusha National Park

Located near Mount Meru, Arusha National Park offers walking safaris, canoeing, and excellent opportunities to see giraffes, zebras, buffaloes, and various primates.

Mount Kilimanjaro

For adventure enthusiasts, we organize professionally guided climbs to Africa's highest mountain. Every expedition is supported by experienced mountain guides, porters, and cooks who prioritize both safety and success.

Zanzibar Beach Holidays

After an exciting safari, guests can relax on Zanzibar's pristine white beaches, explore historical Stone Town, indulge in spice tours, and enjoy world-class snorkeling and diving in turquoise waters.
