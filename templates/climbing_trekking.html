MOUNTAIN CLIMBING & TREKKING

Conquer Africa's Highest Peaks

Tanzania is home to majestic roof-of-Africa summits and dramatic Great Rift Valley stratovolcanoes. Whether you are looking for a non-technical walk-up to Africa's highest point or a remote active caldera trek, our professional mountain expeditions are fully supported by certified guides, porters, and mountain cooks.

Mount Kilimanjaro (The Roof of Africa)

The highest free-standing mountain in the world at 5,895m. A legendary non-technical trek offering diverse climatic zones ranging from lush rainforests to arctic glacial summits.

Mount Meru (The Scenic Warm-up)

Standing at 4,566m inside Arusha National Park, Meru offers dramatic ridge walks, rich wildlife encounters, and incredible panoramic views of Kilimanjaro.

Rift Valley Volcanic Trails (Off the Beaten Path)

Raw and challenging wilderness adventures like Ol Doinyo Lengai and the Crater Highlands, tailored for rugged explorers looking for unique terrain.
