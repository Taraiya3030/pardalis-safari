Experience the thrilling migration of over 1.5 million wildebeest, zebras, and gazelles as they move across the Serengeti ecosystem in search of fresh grazing grounds. Our specialized seasonal itineraries position you right where the action happens.

Seasonal Migration Highlights

River Crossings (July – October)

Watch dramatic Mara and Grumeti river crossings where massive herds brave crocodile-infested waters.

Calving Season in Ndutu (January – March)

Witness thousands of wildebeest calves born daily in the southern Serengeti plains, attracting top apex predators.

Central Serengeti Plains (April – June)

Track the columns of migrating herds as they move north across the central valley and woodland corridors.
