CLASSIC SAFARI VEHICLE

Built for the African Wild. Designed for Your Comfort.

At Leopard Tours, our Classic Safari Vehicle is more than just transport — it is an essential part of your safari experience. Specially designed for Tanzania's national parks and rugged terrain, our 4x4 safari vehicles combine reliability, comfort, and optimal wildlife viewing, ensuring every game drive is smooth, safe, and unforgettable.

This vehicle is ideal for travellers seeking an authentic safari experience with excellent visibility, professional driving, and dependable performance across all terrains.

Vehicle Overview

Our Classic Safari Vehicle is based on a 4x4 Land Cruiser platform, purpose-built for safari operations in Tanzania. Each vehicle is carefully maintained and customized to meet the demands of long game drives, rough tracks, and changing weather conditions across parks such as Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Tarangire, Lake Manyara, and Nduku.

  • Vehicle Type: 4x4 Safari Land Cruiser
  • Seating Capacity:
    • 6 passengers in the main cabin
    • 1 passenger seat next to the driver-guide
  • Usage: Game drives, long safaris, and off-road travel

Designed for Game Viewing & Photography

Wildlife viewing is at the heart of every safari, and our vehicles are designed to ensure you never miss a moment.

Key viewing features include:

  • Pop-up roof offering 360-degree game viewing and photography access
  • Roof access available both in the main passenger cabin and the front seat next to the driver-guide
  • Wide sliding windows for clear unobstructed sightseeing and photography from all seats
  • Elevated seating position for improved visibility across open plains and bushland

Whether you are spotting lions in the Serengeti or elephants in Tarangire, every seat offers a great view.

Comfort & On-Board Facilities

Long game drives require comfort — especially on uneven safari tracks. Our Classic Safari Vehicles are equipped to ensure a pleasant journey throughout the day.

Comfort features include:

  • Standard safari suspension system to handle rough terrain
  • Extended body design providing generous legroom and seating space
  • Air-conditioned cabin for both passengers and driver-guide
  • Mini refrigerator to keep drinks cool during game drives
  • Charging sockets for phones, cameras, and electronic devices
